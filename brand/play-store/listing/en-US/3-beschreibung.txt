PromptAir is a teleprompter that listens: while you speak, the text follows your voice – word by word, at your own pace. If you pause, it waits. If you speed up, it keeps up.

No account. No subscription. No watermark. No character limit.

VOICE CONTROL
• Text follows voice: the reading line jumps to the word you are speaking
• Voice commands: “start”, “stop”, “faster”, “slower”, “restart”
• Prefers on-device recognition (Android 12 and later) – your audio stays with you
• Stage directions in <angle brackets> are skipped while following

REMOTE-STYLE CONTROLS
• Control pad: play/pause in the centre, faster and slower, microphone and recording at your fingertips
• Pinch with two fingers to change the font size, tap to pause
• Works with Bluetooth presenters and keyboards
• Swipe back to leave the prompter

CAMERA AND RECORDING
• Front or rear camera behind semi-transparent text
• Records video without the overlay text, saved straight to “Movies/PromptAir”
• No watermark, no length limit

FULLY ADJUSTABLE
• Speed, font size, line spacing, margins, font, alignment
• Text and background colour, reading line and focus dimming
• Horizontal and vertical mirroring for prompter rigs
• Countdown, loop, keep screen on
• Time remaining and progress bar

READ ALOUD
• Your device’s offline text-to-speech reads your script – for rehearsing and checking your timing
• Choose voice and speed, add pauses with [pause] and [pause lang]
• Share as a WAV file

SCRIPTS
• As many scripts as you like, no length limit
• Load .txt files and save them as .txt wherever you choose
• Emphasis with **bold**

SIX LANGUAGES
Interface and voice commands in English, German, Spanish, French, Portuguese and Italian.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
PromptAir has no internet permission. Scripts, settings and videos stay on your device only – no account, no server, no ads, no tracking.
