3. No cookies, no tracking, no accounts

This website and the web app do not use cookies, analytics services, advertising networks or social media plugins. There is no registration and there are no user accounts. Fonts and all other resources are loaded without connections to third parties.

4. PromptAir web app (browser)

Scripts and settings

The texts you enter and your settings are stored exclusively in your browser’s local storage (localStorage) on your device. They are not transmitted to us or to third parties. You can remove them at any time by deleting the browser data for this website.

Camera and microphone

Camera and microphone are only used after you have explicitly allowed them in your browser’s permission dialog (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, consent). Video recordings are created and saved directly on your device; they are not transmitted to us. You can withdraw the permission at any time in your browser’s site settings.

Voice control (Web Speech API)

If you switch on the optional voice control, the web app uses your browser’s speech recognition. With common browsers (e.g. Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge) the recorded audio may be sent to servers of the browser vendor (e.g. Google or Microsoft) and processed there for recognition. We have no influence on this; the browser vendor’s privacy policy applies. Voice control is optional – the prompter is fully usable without it.

5. PromptAir Android app

Principle: processing on your device only

The Android app works entirely locally and has no internet permission. It therefore cannot transmit any content to us or to third parties. The app contains no ads, no analytics or tracking libraries and does not create accounts. Scripts and settings are stored only in the app’s storage on your device and are excluded from cloud backups.

Permissions

The microphone (voice control, video sound) and the camera (video recording) are only used after you have explicitly agreed in the Android system dialog (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw these permissions at any time in the system settings.

Saved files

Videos you record are saved by the app in the folder “Movies/PromptAir”; exported scripts are saved to the location you choose yourself when saving. These files are under your sole control.

Speech recognition

For voice control the app uses your Android system’s speech recognition. From Android 12 onwards it prefers on-device recognition; the audio does not leave your device. If no offline language pack is available on your device for the selected language, the app tells you and uses your system’s speech recognition service (usually Google). This service may send the audio to the provider’s servers for recognition; the provider’s privacy policy applies. Voice control is optional – the prompter is fully usable without it.

Read aloud

The read-aloud feature uses your system’s text-to-speech with its offline voices directly on the device. The generated speech is synthetic.

6. No disclosure of data

We do not sell data and do not pass any data on to third parties. Since we do not collect any data ourselves, we hold no personal data about you beyond the technical hosting log data mentioned in section 2.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). You can withdraw any consent you have given at any time with effect for the future. Please contact us at the address above. Because PromptAir stores your content only locally, you can usually exercise erasure and data portability yourself: using the delete functions in the app and browser and the export functions of the app.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the authority responsible for your place of residence.

8. Changes to this policy

We update this privacy policy when features of the website or the apps change. The version published here applies.
